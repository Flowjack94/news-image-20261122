A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026, drawn entirely in code.

**Lead story:** COP31 climate talks deadlocked as the fight over fossil fuels stalls a deal — delegates from nearly 200 countries at an impasse.

**Also on the page:** Iran strikes a U.S. air base in Kuwait as Gulf attacks widen (tankers hit near Oman, an ADNOC vessel attacked in the Strait of Hormuz); a magnitude 6.9 earthquake shakes Peru's Andes; drones hit a Russian cargo ship in the Baltic; Trump in Shenzhen for the APEC summit with Xi and Putin; India face New Zealand in the Wellington Test.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, built-ins only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, byte for byte, every run. The image itself was generated for this entry with a Python/PIL script in the build session and is held in this repository.
